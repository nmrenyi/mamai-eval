Generator faithfulness to context: Gemma 4 vs Gemma 3n, across prompt arms

2026-06-22 · config-v0.2.0 · oracle mamaretrieval v0.2.0 (score≥5, top-3, n=2989) · generators Gemma 4 E4B (Q4_0) & Gemma 3n E4B (Q4_0) · judge pipeline Patronus Lynx-70B → gpt-5 categorize+calibrate (Option B) · English

What this measures. Each model was handed the gold guideline passages (oracle retrieval, not live RAG) for 2,989 maternal/neonatal questions, and asked to answer. Faithfulness = does the answer stay inside what the context supports? A failure is a contradiction (wrong dose / threshold / claim) or an unsupported addition (clinical claim not in the context). Incompleteness and refusals are not faithfulness failures. This isolates the generator's grounding behaviour from retrieval quality.
Bottom line.
  • Gemma 4 is ~2× more faithful than Gemma 3n at every prompt level. On the conservative, full-population categorized rate: G4 sits at 2.6–3.6% true-hallucination vs 3n's 6.3–6.7% — a 1.9–2.4× gap that holds for baseline, +G1, and +G1+G2.
  • The prompt's faithfulness cost is generator-dependent. The deflection-fix prompts (G1, then +G2 structure) push both models to add more un-grounded content, but Gemma 4 absorbs it (categorized 2.64→3.55%) while Gemma 3n was already the weaker grounder and stays high (6.26→6.72%).
  • Both prompts trade refusals for unsupported additions. G1 does what it was designed to do — fewer deflections — but the recovered answers include more claims beyond the document. That is the mechanism behind the faithfulness cost.
  • This corroborates the earlier branch finding (g1-prompt-ab-outcome) that prompt-only deflection fixes hit a grounding ceiling on 3n, and reframes the lever as RAG-grounding, not prompt tuning.

2 · The defensible metric: categorized true-hallucination

The categorized rate is the number to trust: it is computed over all 2,989 responses (no extrapolation), and it strips out the things Lynx over-flags — incompleteness and refusals — keeping only real contradictions and unsupported additions. On this metric the ranking is unambiguous and stable across prompts:

Both models drift upward as the prompt asks for more clinical substance — G4 by +0.9pp (2.64→3.55), 3n by +0.5pp (6.26→6.72) — but the increments are small and the generator gap dwarfs the prompt effect. Choosing the on-device generator matters roughly 4× more for faithfulness than choosing the prompt.

  • G1 converts refusals into unsupported additions. For Gemma 4, refusal FAILs fall 27→7 while unsupported additions rise 28→55: the prompt successfully stops the model deflecting, and the substance it now volunteers sometimes reaches beyond the document. This is the deflection-fix working and its price, in one row.
  • 3n carries a much larger contradiction load at baseline (91 vs G4's 51) — it gets clinical facts wrong against the very context it was given almost twice as often. That is the core grounding deficit, present before any prompt change.
  • 3n's raw FAIL inflation under G1 is mostly non-hallucination. 3n's raw rate jumps 8.6→11.1%, but much of that is a surge in refusal-category FAILs (14→103) that Lynx penalises — not genuine hallucination. That is exactly why 3n's categorized rate barely moves (6.26→6.46%) even as its raw rate climbs sharply, and why the raw number is misleading on its own.

4 · The calibrated estimate — and why it is the noisy column

Lynx is a high-recall / low-precision detector: it both over-flags (precision 0.36–0.57) and under-detects. The calibrated rate corrects for the under-detection by sampling 50 Lynx-PASS answers per arm, re-judging them with gpt-5, and extrapolating the miss-rate across the ~2,700–2,850 PASS population. Because that population is huge, the estimate is dominated by a miss-rate measured on only 50 cases — so its confidence intervals are wide and a few sampled cases swing it a lot.

Two reasons to treat calibrated rates as an upper-bound band, not a point estimate:
  • The g4_g1g2 outlier (†). Its 53.9% comes from a miss-rate point estimate of 0.54 (27 of 50 PASS cases re-judged FAIL) — far above its neighbours (0.18, 0.22) and with a CI of [0.42–0.68]. One unusually strict 50-case draw, not a 2.5× collapse in grounding between +G1 and +G1+G2.
  • Same model, same data, calibrated rate moved 9%→19%. An earlier Gemma 4 oracle run (oracle-v0.2.0-faithfulness) scored categorized 2.51% / calibrated 9.05% (miss-rate 0.08); this run's baseline scored categorized 2.64% / calibrated 19.1% (miss-rate 0.18). The categorized numbers agree to within 0.1pp; the calibrated numbers differ 2× purely from the 50-sample miss-rate draw. The categorized metric is reproducible; the calibrated metric is not, at this sample size.

What the calibrated column does robustly show — surviving its noise — is the same two facts as the categorized column: (a) Gemma 3n > Gemma 4 in hallucination at every arm (27→46→47% vs 19→23%, ignoring the g1g2 spike), and (b) prompts push the rate up, more so on 3n. It simply states them with wider error bars and a larger amplitude. Where the two metrics disagree is magnitude: the calibrated view makes "+G1 nearly doubles 3n's hallucination" (27→46%), whereas the categorized view says the genuine-hallucination increase is mild (6.26→6.46%). The mild reading is the trustworthy one; the doubling is largely Lynx-miss-rate noise plus the refusal surge of §3.

5 · Interpretation

For the device decision. On faithfulness alone, Gemma 4 is the safer grounder by a clear ~2× margin, and it tolerates the deflection-fix prompts without a meaningful grounding penalty. Gemma 3n — the currently-deployed generator — pays for its size with roughly double the genuine-hallucination rate against gold context, independent of prompt. No prompt in this study closes that gap; G1/G2 modestly widen it.

This is the faithfulness half of the picture. It must be read against the end-to-end track (recall / safety / rubric, +RAG on kenya & healthbench), where G1's deflection fix raises clinical recall — the upside that motivated it. The faithfulness cost quantified here is the counterweight: the extra substance G1 elicits is partly un-grounded. The path that improves recall without the grounding tax is RAG-grounding of the generator (G-RAG), not further prompt tuning — consistent with the retriever/generator synthesis and the G1 prompt-A/B outcome on this branch.

6 · Method

  • Stimulus. Oracle context = mamaretrieval v0.2.0, relevance score ≥5, top-3 passages, n=2,989 questions. Identical context fed to both generators and all three prompt arms, so any difference is the generator/prompt, never retrieval.
  • Generators. Gemma 4 E4B and Gemma 3n E4B, both Q4_0 GGUF (the on-device quantization), greedy/config-pinned decoding. Prompts: baseline (deployed), +G1 (deflection/scope fix), +G1+G2 (G1 plus consultation-workflow structure) — the same three arm prompts used elsewhere on this branch.
  • Detector. Patronus Lynx-70B (TP=2) → PASS/FAIL with reasoning.
  • Categorize (gpt-5, Option B). Every FAIL re-judged by gpt-5 against a frozen rubric into contradiction / unsupported-addition / omission / refusal / unclear; the first two are counted as true hallucination. Full-population, deterministic.
  • Calibrate (gpt-5). Per arm, a seeded stratified blind sample (50 PASS + up to 10 per FAIL category, n≈96–100) re-judged from first principles → Lynx precision, miss-rate, and a population estimate with bootstrap 95% CIs.
  • Provenance. 3n arms from g1-ab-3n-20260619/faith (2026-06-19); G4 arms generated 2026-06-22 (gen-prompt-matrix-20260622/faith) on the EPFL cluster; identical pipeline.

7 · Caveats & scope

  • Oracle, not RAG. This is faithfulness-to-gold-context — an upper bound on grounding behaviour. Under live retrieval, missing/wrong passages add a separate error mode not measured here.
  • English only; Swahili faithfulness untested.
  • Calibrated rates are 50-PASS-sample extrapolations with wide CIs (§4) — directional, not precise. Treat categorized as the metric of record.
  • "unclear" cases (6–13 per arm) are excluded from true-hallucination by construction; they are a small, bounded source of undercount.
  • Single judge family for the semantic steps (gpt-5). The judge-validation plan (judge-validation-plan) against physician ground truth still applies before these rates are pinned as absolute.
