G1/G2 Generator Faithfulness (oracle context) — Gemma 3n E4B × arm1/2/3

2026-06-19 · config-v0.2.0 · generator Gemma 3n E4B (Q4_0) · oracle mamaretrieval v0.2.0 (score ≥ 5, top-3, 2,989 q) · scorer Patronus Lynx 70B · categorize+calibrate gpt-5 Batch. Parallels reports/oracle-v0.2.0-faithfulness.html (which measured Gemma 4).

Bottom line. Two findings, both adverse and both consistent with the no-RAG SAQ result. (1) Gemma 3n is far less faithful to retrieved context than Gemma 4 — calibrated true-hallucination ~27% (arm1 baseline) vs Gemma 4's 9.05%. (2) The G1 prompt makes it worse: calibrated 27% → 46% (arm2) → 47% (arm3) — G1 is the hit, the +G2 structure adds little — driven by Lynx's miss-rate nearly doubling (26% → 46%) — i.e. G1 adds stealth unfaithful content Lynx passes but the strict gpt-5 rubric catches. The categorized rate alone hid this (≈flat 6.3% → 6.5% → 6.7%); only the Option-B calibration revealed it. This is the faithfulness leg of the gate the no-RAG SAQ harm metric could only proxy — and it fails.

1. Method (identical to the Gemma-4 oracle report, new variables)

Each arm's prompt generates one answer per oracle query given the top-3 known-relevant guideline chunks, so any unfaithfulness is the generator's (not retrieval's). Lynx 70B gives a holistic PASS/FAIL; gpt-5 then (a) categorizes the FAILs (only contradiction + unsupported_addition are real hallucination; omission/refusal are not) and (b) calibrates Lynx on a stratified blind sample (PASS + FAIL) to correct both false alarms and Lynx's misses, yielding a population estimate. Three rates per arm: raw Lynx FAIL · categorized true-hallucination · calibrated true-hallucination.

3. Why the categorized and calibrated numbers diverge — and why it matters

Reading raw Lynx alone (8.6% → 11.1%) would say "G1 hurts faithfulness," but most of that jump is the refusal bucket (14 → 103 FAILs), not hallucination. Reading categorized alone (6.26% → 6.46%) would say "G1 barely changes hallucination." Both are wrong. The calibration is decisive: G1 roughly doubles Lynx's miss rate (26% → 46%), meaning the extra content G1 elicits (drugs, management, escalation, referral not in the context) includes a large share of unsupported additions that Lynx's holistic judge waves through but gpt-5's strict §3.1 rubric flags. Net calibrated hallucination 27% → 46%.

An oracle-vs-SAQ contrast worth noting. In the no-RAG SAQ A/B, G1 eliminated deflection. Here, with context, G1 increased the refusal-flagged FAILs (14 → 103). The likely mechanism: G1's "manage-then-refer / escalate / address-the-clinician" levers make 3n append referral and escalation that isn't grounded in the provided chunk, which the context-faithfulness judge penalizes. So the very levers that make answers more engaging also make them less grounded.

4. Verdict

The faithfulness gate — previously only proxied by the SAQ safety enum — is now measured, and it fails for G1 on 3n, consistent with every other signal (SAQ harm 22%→35%, dangerous 4→9). Two layered conclusions:

  • 3n itself has a grounding problem. Even the baseline prompt hallucinates against known-good context at ~3× the Gemma-4 rate (27% vs 9% calibrated). This is a property of the deployed generator, independent of the A/B — and the strongest evidence yet that generator RAG-grounding is the real lever (the R2c synthesis recommendation), not retrieval or prompting.
  • G1/G2 make grounding worse, not better. Pushing 3n to engage more adds unsupported content. Combined with the SAQ harm regression, this confirms: do not ship G1/G2 as-is on 3n.

5. Caveats

  • Calibration samples are small (n≈96–99) → wide CIs; treat the calibrated point estimates as directional, not precise. The categorized rates (full n) are tighter and show ≈flat real-hallucination — the disagreement between "flat categorized" and "rising calibrated" is itself the finding (Lynx-miss growth).
  • Different distribution from the no-RAG SAQ arms: this is the with-context oracle set, the only setting in which faithfulness is defined. It complements, not replaces, the SAQ safety signal.
  • Gemma 3n is the Q4_0 cluster proxy for on-device int4. gpt-5 is the calibration judge (the prior G4 report used the same methodology with a gpt-5 calibrator, so the 3n-vs-G4 comparison is apples-to-apples).
